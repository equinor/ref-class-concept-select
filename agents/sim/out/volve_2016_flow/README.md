# Volve deck for OPM Flow

`VOLVE_2016_FLOW.DATA` is the released Eclipse 100 deck `VOLVE_2016.DATA` with
only the changes OPM Flow needs in order to run it. It runs to completion in
Flow 2026.04 with default options.

The new files are beside the originals in
`$VOLVE_DATA/eclipse_model/sim_model_PPA-eclipse/`. No original file is
modified; unchanged include files are shared with the Eclipse deck.

| New file | Version of | Change |
|---|---|---|
| `VOLVE_2016_FLOW.DATA` | `VOLVE_2016.DATA` | 10 edits, listed below |
| `GRID_postF1B_Nov2013_locupd_12112013_FLOW.grdecl` | `GRID_postF1B_Nov2013_locupd_12112013.grdecl` | 12 600 of 5 443 200 `ZCORN` values shifted (7 182 lines); everything else byte-identical |
| `RSVD_input_new_combined_020610_perch_water_2914m_FLOW.E100` | `RSVD_input_…_2914m.E100` | 13th table commented out |
| `SCH_010916_10DAYS_FLOW.SCH` | `SCH_010916_10DAYS.SCH` | Five records commented out |

The files are generated by `simkit.flow_deck` (`agents/sim/src/simkit/`), which
holds every edit with its reason and refuses to run unless the originals match
`data/manifest.csv`. In the generated files each change is marked `-- FLOW:`
and each disabled original line is kept as a `--FLOW` comment.

```bash
make flow-deck       # write the four files
make flow-run        # mpirun -np 8 flow …; output in agents/sim/work/volve_2016_flow/run/
make flow-compare    # compare with the shipped Eclipse results
```

## The changes

Line numbers refer to the original files.

| # | File, lines | Change | Why it is needed |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | `.DATA` 620 | 13th `ROCK` record (`329 2.0E-5 / #13`) disabled | `TABDIMS` declares 12 regions. Flow stops on it. Eclipse ignored it ("spurious data" in `VOLVE_2016.LOG`) |
| 2 | `RSVD…E100` 236–256; `.DATA` 806 points to the new file | 13th `RSVD` table disabled | `EQLDIMS` declares 12 regions. Flow stops on it. Eclipse ignored it as spurious data |
| 3 | `SCH_010916…SCH` 4869–4873; `.DATA` 981 points to the new file | Five well-control records after `DATES 1 JAN 2015` disabled | They have no `WCONHIST` keyword. Flow stops on them. Eclipse ignored them as spurious data |
| 4 | `.DATA` 99 | `EQLOPTS 'THPRES' 'QUIESC' 'MOBILE'` → `'THPRES'` | Flow does not implement `QUIESC` and `MOBILE` and stops on them |
| 5 | `.DATA` 941–942 | `PRIORITY` disabled | Flow does not implement it and stops. The deck has no prioritisation group control (`GCONPRI`), so it has no effect |
| 6 | `.DATA` 480–481, 486–487, 492–493, 498–499 disabled; 179 points to the new grid file | The four `ADDZCORN` includes are replaced by a grid file with those shifts applied | Flow does not implement `ADDZCORN` and stops on it |

Changes 1–3 reproduce what Eclipse did. Change 6 is equivalent by
construction: the shifts are the differences between the input `ZCORN` and the
corner depths Eclipse wrote to `VOLVE_2016.GRID`. They are whole metres from
−20 to +90, in the area i 58–70, j 43–61, all layers, and are listed in
`zcorn_shifts.csv`. Changes 4 and 5 remove features Flow lacks.

Keywords that Flow does not implement but only warns about are left as they
are: `GRIDFILE`, `RPTGRID`, `RPTSOL`, `RPTRUNSP`, `NSTACK`, `PARALLEL`,
`ECHO`/`NOECHO`, two `MESSAGES` options, and twelve summary vectors.

`changes.diff` is the full difference for the three text files.

## Verification

Flow 2026.04 (`libopm-simulators-bin` 2026.04-1~noble), `mpirun -np 8`, on
2026-11-20. Compared with the shipped Eclipse 2015.1 results using `resdata`.
Evidence: `compare_init.csv`, `compare_summary.csv`, `compare_balance.csv`.

**Static model.** Active cells are the same 183 545. Cell depth, DX, DY, DZ,
pore volume, porosity, net-to-gross, permeabilities, transmissibility
multipliers and region numbers are identical in every active cell. The
non-neighbour connections are the same 29 301 cell pairs.

**Run.** Completes: 465 time steps, 11 minutes on 8 processes. 28 time steps
were cut after convergence failures and repeated. Two runs gave identical
results.

**Material balance.** Water in place differs from initial + injected −
produced by 86 Sm3 at the end, oil by −82 Sm3 (Eclipse: 44 and −40 Sm3).

## Known differences from the Eclipse model

1. **Order of two `EDIT` operations.** The deck sets `MULTZ` to 0.1 in layers
   20, 22 and 23 (i 60–90, j 38–57) and caps `TRANZ` at 2000. Eclipse caps and
   then multiplies (at most 200); Flow multiplies and then caps (at most
   2000). `TRANZ` therefore differs in about 1 450 cells of those layers, by a
   factor of up to 10. This is the largest known difference and is not
   corrected; see the trial below.
2. **Pinch-outs.** `TRANZ` differs in 143 cells above pinched-out cells; in 84
   of them Eclipse has zero and Flow does not.
3. **Faults.** `TRANX` and `TRANY` differ by more than 0.01 % in 697 and 688
   cells, nearly all at faults, mostly by less than 1 % and at most 11 %,
   apart from 7 cells where Eclipse has zero. Of the 29 301 non-neighbour
   transmissibilities, 4 906 differ by more than 1 %; their sums agree within
   0.02 %.
4. **Equilibration.** Without `QUIESC` and `MOBILE` the initial state could
   differ; initial oil in place and field pressure agree as shown above.
5. **Region 9 pressure** ends at 367 bar in Flow against 327 bar in Eclipse.
   The other ten regions agree within 2 bar. Not investigated.
6. **Smaller items.** Flow uses oil instead of liquid as preferred phase for
   eight wells; 36 well connections lie in inactive cells and are ignored
   (Eclipse also warns of connections to dead blocks).

## Trial: correcting the `EDIT` order

Adding a second cap (`MAXVALUE TRANZ 200` on the three layers) makes Flow's
`TRANZ` equal Eclipse's in those cells (1 595 differing cells become 143) and
brings the cumulative volumes closer: oil −0.17 %, water +0.19 %, gas −0.24 %.
But that run does not conserve mass: by the end 20 676 Sm3 of water and
21 435 Sm3 of oil are unaccounted for, growing steadily from about day 1000,
and field pressure ends 7.5 bar (2.2 %) below Eclipse. The cause was not
established. The cap is therefore not in the deck. Evidence:
`tranz_cap_trial/`.

Not tried: the same trial in a serial run, or expressing the correction as
`MULTIPLY TRANZ 0.1` after the existing cap instead of a second cap.
